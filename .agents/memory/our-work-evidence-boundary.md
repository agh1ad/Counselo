---
name: Our Work evidence boundary
description: How CounselO case studies may support trust and search meaning without becoming fabricated reviews or ratings.
---

CounselO’s published Our Work items are publisher-authored, redacted case studies. They may support CreativeWork/CaseStudy-style discovery, service scope, jurisdiction, authorship, and documented methodology, but they are not independent client reviews and must not produce Review or aggregateRating markup.

**Why:** The published narratives and redacted documents establish the nature and limits of the work described, not a customer’s independent satisfaction or a representative score.

**How to apply:** Keep the evidence boundary visible on collection and detail pages. Add client feedback only when it is genuinely supplied and publication consent is recorded; then represent that feedback separately from case-study data.
