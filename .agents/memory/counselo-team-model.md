name: CounselO one-team service model
description: Owner-confirmed relationship between CounselO's central legal team and participating local lawyers and offices.
---

Clients come to one CounselO legal team for advice and representation in Saudi Arabia, Syria, and the UAE. Participating lawyers and offices in each country are members of that team. A client may request advice alone or continue with the same team for representation.

The team model does not mean CounselO owns every participating office or holds a shared licence. Local legal authority remains with the appropriately licensed lawyer or office. Confirm required authorization, scope, and fees before representation or other reserved work proceeds.

**Why:** The owner clarified the service relationship so public copy distinguishes team membership from local licensing authority.

**How to apply:** Describe one team and continued service across stages; avoid referral, handoff, or consultation-only framing. Keep jurisdiction-specific identities and licensing boundaries explicit, and do not imply that an office's directory record identifies CounselO.
