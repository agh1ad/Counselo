## Search intent

All pages have a recorded primary purpose and supporting intents in [the page ledger](page-seo-ledger-2026-09-06.json). Of the 520 supplied distinct Keyword Planner phrases, **435 map to appropriate destinations**, **85 have explicit exclusions**, and none is unresolved. The follow-up restored 11 useful selection, credential-verification and response-time mappings without claiming superiority, nationality, a free service or round-the-clock availability.

Exclusions cover unsupported offers, unrelated jurisdictions, some official-navigation queries, irrelevant training/employment intents and ambiguous terms without a defensible destination. They are not omitted website pages. English destinations are language counterparts, not evidence of measured English demand; unsegmented keyword volumes are not attributed to a country or treated as observed rankings.

Google prioritizes useful, reliable, people-first content; Bing also emphasizes clarity and avoiding low-value duplication. Those principles support clear page purposes and relevant consolidation, not a separate page for every spelling or a promise of first position. [Google guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content), [Bing guidelines](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a).
