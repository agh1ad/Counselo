# CounselO Homepage Canvas Audit

**Audit date:** 2026-08-09

## Scope

Reviewed the rendered visual canvas for the regional homepage layout in English and Arabic, including the desktop hero composition, RTL mirroring, typography, map artwork, navigation, CTA placement, and responsive CSS rules.

## Box-by-box result

- **Easy to read:** Pass. The canvas has a clear left/right reading path in English and correctly mirrors for Arabic. The headline is the dominant visual element, followed by supporting copy and actions.
- **Fast to read:** Pass. The hero exposes the jurisdiction, service, audience, channels, and two actions without requiring scrolling. The map is a visual anchor rather than a competing text block.
- **Clear:** Pass. The current-jurisdiction bar, regional map, headline, service highlights, and CTA labels make the page context immediately identifiable.
- **Direct:** Pass. “Start a Consultation” and “Our Practice Areas” are visually distinct and appear before the fold; the navigation also carries a consultation action.
- **Professional:** Pass. The forest-green, white, and gold system, restrained line-art/map treatment, serif headline, bilingual RTL mirroring, and legal-scope language present a credible legal-service canvas.

## Visual checks completed

- Saudi Arabia English rendered desktop hero inspected.
- Saudi Arabia Arabic rendered RTL hero inspected.
- Regional map and decorative geometry remain subordinate to the headline and actions.
- Desktop and responsive breakpoint rules keep the hero as a one-column flow on small screens, with CTA stacking and the map moved below the copy.
- No overlapping primary CTA, broken RTL direction, or illegible hero text was observed in the inspected canvases.

## Conclusion

The homepage canvas passes all five criteria. It is intentionally editorial and premium rather than minimal or utilitarian; the visual treatment supports trust without displacing the primary message or conversion path.
