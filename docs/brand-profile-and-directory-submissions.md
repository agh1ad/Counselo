**English description**

CounselO Legal is an online legal platform founded by lawyer and legal counsel Omar Al-Baghdadi. Its member law firms serve Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates and Syria. Each firm has a team of licensed lawyers who are part of CounselO Legal. The platform and its founder independently verify those lawyers’ professional experience. Clients consult in Arabic or English, share documents and follow their matters online. Locally licensed lawyers handle representation in the relevant jurisdiction.
