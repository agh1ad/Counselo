# Preparing a CounselO work publication

Use the editable [Arabic template](../templates/work-publication-ar.html) or
[English template](../templates/work-publication-en.html). Keep the shared
`work-publication.css` beside the HTML file. Open the HTML in Chrome, edit the
bracketed fields, and use **Save edited HTML** to retain the draft or **Print /
Save PDF** to export it. In the print dialog, use A4, turn on background graphics
and turn off the browser's own headers and footers. The publication footer is
included by the template.

The approved service line is **Online Consultation to Legal Representation** /
**من الاستشارة أونلاين إلى التمثيل القانوني**. It describes one CounselO team of
lawyers and law offices. Online consultation remains prominent; the wording does
not label courtroom work as an entirely online service. Credit the named lawyer
or member office for the work they actually performed.

Start with the recorded result. Preserve the case jurisdiction, original work
date, amounts, procedural stage and source attribution. Use a separate edition
date for publication updates. A case study is a CounselO publication; the template
does not certify it as a court-issued document. Replace every placeholder and
complete confidentiality and factual review before upload. Check every exported
page, including logo images, footers, tables and Arabic text order, and prepare a
matching readable text edition.

These templates are private authoring resources, not public sample cases. Uploads
retain their supplied PDF content; the application does not automatically apply
the template or rewrite their branding. Follow the existing
[guarded document edition workflow](public-work-document-indexing.md) for a
revision of an already published file, preserving its original source metadata
and stable PDF/TXT URLs.
